During this reporting period, I continued developing the documentation related to my internship project, with a particular focus on the design and formalization of the scientific protocol that will guide the study.

As part of this process, an ethics application was prepared and submitted to the CERUM (Université de Montréal Research Ethics Committee) to obtain approval for the experimental protocol prior to the start of data collection.

In parallel, I began exploring the technological capabilities already available at the internship site in order to assess potential options for data acquisition and measurement. This preliminary investigation revealed that the bowling system currently in use internally calculates ball speed during each delivery. Although this information is not displayed to the general public, it may be accessible for research purposes and could provide a valuable source of data for the project.
